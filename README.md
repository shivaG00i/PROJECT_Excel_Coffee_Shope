# PROJECT_Excel_Coffee_Shope

📊 Project Overview

This project is a sales analysis dashboard for a fictional coffee shop. The goal is to derive actionable business insights from transactional sales data using Excel Pivot Tables and Charts. The project helps identify performance trends, customer behavior, and key sales drivers to support strategic business decisions.

🎯 Business Objectives
The main objectives of this project are:
Analyze retail sales data to identify peak sales periods.
Understand customer purchase patterns by day and hour.
Identify top-performing products and categories.
Evaluate store location performance.
Calculate key performance metrics like average bill and orders per person.
Visualize insights using a clear and interactive dashboard.


📁 Data Description
The dataset includes:
transaction_id: Unique transaction identifier
transaction_qty: Quantity of items sold
Total_Bill: Total revenue for each transaction
Store Location: Branch where the sale occurred
Product Name, Category, Size
Date and Hour of Sale


✅ Key Questions Answered
#	Business Question	Insights Visualized
1	How do sales vary by day and hour?	Orders by Day of Week & Quantity by Hour
2	Are there any peak sales times?	Peak order hours & busiest days
3	What is the total monthly revenue?	Monthly Total Sales
4	How do sales vary by store location?	Sales & Footfall by Location
5	What is the average bill and orders per person?	KPI Cards
6	What are the best-selling products by revenue?	Top 5 Products
7	How do sales vary by product category and size?	Category Distribution & Size Distribution
